=====================================================================

The marker must look BROAD, THICK and DARK.

Target visual impression:

The strokes must look:

- thick
- dark
- bold
- confident
- firm
- substantial
- hand-drawn
- slightly organic
- consistent

=====================================================================

The marker should have a FIRM, CONTROLLED, MEDIUM-HARD CHARACTER.

- soft
- fuzzy
- feathered
- bleeding
- charcoal-like

- brush-painted

THICK + DARK + FIRM + HAND-DRAWN.
